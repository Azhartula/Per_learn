One loop. Continuously re-measured.

We do not label children with learning styles. We measure which method reaches real mastery for this child right now — and we change as they grow.

1 · Diagnose Adaptive quiz estimates level and flags misconceptions.
2 · Choose method A contextual bandit picks among four teaching approaches.
3 · Generate lesson Lesson is written for that method; every answer is SymPy-checked.
4 · Delayed check Unassisted review becomes the reward — practice scores never train the policy.

Across many children

In simulation on ASSISTments fraction parameters, the contextual bandit reaches mastery faster than fixed or random method assignment.
