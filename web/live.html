# Ordering and comparing fractions correctly **Method:** worked_example **Skill:** ordering_fractions **Misconception targeted:** Thinks a bigger denominator means a bigger fraction **Source:** ollama **Model:** qwen2.5:3b **All answers SymPy-verified:** yes ## Hook Let's look at a clear example to understand how to compare fractions. ## Worked example Order the fractions 1/3 and 2/5 from smallest to largest. 1. Find a common denominator for 1/3 and 2/5. The least common multiple of 3 and 5 is 15. 2. Convert 1/3 to a fraction with a denominator of 15: 1/3 = 5/15. 3. Convert 2/5 to a fraction with a denominator of 15: 2/5 = 6/15. 4. Now compare 5/15 and 6/15. Since 5 < 6, 1/3 < 2/5. 5. Therefore, the order from smallest to largest is 1/3, 2/5. **Answer:** 1/3, 2/5 ## Misconception check Remember, a bigger denominator does not mean the fraction is bigger. The size of the fraction depends on both the numerator and the denominator. ## Practice - (ord_03) Which is greater: 2/5 or 3/7? → `3/7` [✓] - (ord_05) True or false: 2/6 is greater than 2/4. → `false` [✓] - (ord_07) Which fraction is closest to 1: 3/4, 2/3, or 1/2? → `3/4` [✓] ## Exit ticket Order the fractions 1/4 and 3/8 from smallest to largest. **Answer:** 1/4, 3/8
