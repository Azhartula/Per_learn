# Ordering and Comparing Fractions **Method:** visual **Skill:** ordering_fractions **Misconception targeted:** Thinks a bigger denominator means a bigger fraction **Source:** ollama **Model:** qwen2.5:7b **All answers SymPy-verified:** yes ## Visual ``` Fraction Bar: 1/3 |-----|-----|-----| 1/2 |-----|-----|-----| 3/7 |-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----| ``` Fraction bars show 1/3, 1/2, and 3/7. Notice how 3/7 is closer to 1 than 1/2, despite having a larger denominator. ## Explanation Fraction bars help us see the size of fractions. A bigger denominator doesn't always mean a bigger fraction. It just means the fraction is divided into more parts. ## Misconception check A bigger denominator means the parts are smaller, so the fraction might be smaller. ## Practice - (ord_03) Which is greater: 2/5 or 3/7? → `3/7` [✓] - (ord_05) True or false: 2/6 is greater than 2/4. → `false` [✓] - (ord_07) Which fraction is closest to 1: 3/4, 2/3, or 1/2? → `3/4` [✓] ## Exit ticket Which is greater: 1/3 or 2/7? **Answer:** 1/3
