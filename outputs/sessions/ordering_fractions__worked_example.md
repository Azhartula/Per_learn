## Worked example

1. Read the problem: Which is larger: 1/3 or 1/4?
2. Same numerator: smaller denominator means larger pieces.
3. So the answer is 1/3.
4. Watch out: Thinks a bigger denominator means a bigger fraction
**Answer:** 1/3
