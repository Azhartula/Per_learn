## Story
Sam is sharing food with friends and needs multiplying fractions. The puzzle is: Calculate 1/2 × 1/3.

## Maths in the story
1. Read the problem: Calculate 1/2 × 1/3.
2. Multiply numerators and denominators: 1×1 / 2×3.
3. So the answer is 1/6.
4. Watch out: Multiplies fractions by adding instead
**Answer:** 1/6

## Misconception check
In the story, don't fall for: Multiplies fractions by adding instead
